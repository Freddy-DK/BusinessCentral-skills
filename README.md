## Skills

### [e-conomic → Business Central migration](economic-to-business-central/README.md)

Migrates a Danish e-conomic bookkeeping into a Business Central company through the Origo Cloud Events connector — chart of accounts, posting setup, VAT, customers, items, transactions, sales invoices and bilag receipts — reconciling to the source exactly, with two PowerShell helpers for the receipt (bilag) side.

**Prerequisites:** Business Central SaaS 28.0+, Origo Cloud Events Core
